⚠️ Mandatory Basic Training Required

Welcome to the volunteer team! Before you can view or accept disaster response tasks, you must review the training materials below.

How the Application Works & Volunteer Manual

1. Your Dashboard

Once activated, your dashboard will display active disaster events, a resource inventory, and any tasks assigned to you by administrators.

2. Disaster Events

Information about ongoing disasters, including severity and location, is updated by admins in real-time. Review these events to understand where help is needed.

3. Resource Allocation

The system tracks available resources like food, medical supplies, and shelter. You may be asked to help manage or distribute these resources safely to the affected areas.

4. Task Assignments

Administrators will assign you specific tasks based on your skills and availability. It is crucial to mark your assignments as completed once done, so the system stays up to date.

5. Safety & Communication

Always follow safety protocols provided by ground coordinators. Maintain open communication with your assigned team lead at all times. Failure to comply may result in removal from the volunteer pool.

6. Emergency Evacuation Procedures

If a staging area becomes unsafe, an emergency evacuation order will be initiated. Volunteers must drop non-essential supplies and follow the designated escape routes immediately. Do not attempt to salvage equipment if your life is at risk.

7. Stress Management & Mental Health

Disaster response can be highly traumatic. We encourage psychological first aid. If you feel overwhelmed, report to your team lead. Taking breaks and managing mental fatigue is a requirement, not a weakness.

8. Volunteer Code of Conduct

Volunteers represent the organization. Discriminatory behavior, failure to distribute resources equitably, or accepting bribes from victims is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate termination.

9. Incident Reporting

If you witness a safety hazard, an injury to another volunteer or victim, or a misuse of resources, you must log an incident report within the application immediately after securing your own safety.
